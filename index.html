01 Selected work

Client names are left out because the work is under NDA.

In-house · Energy

Building a security programme from a reactive start

Joined an energy group where security meant putting out fires. Set up central log monitoring, a vulnerability register where every finding has an ID, an owner and an age, and regular re-testing so fixes get checked instead of assumed.

A re-test showed a remote desktop service had been closed at the firewall, and surfaced a database that was still exposed months after first reported. It went back to leadership with a plain explanation of the risk.

In-house · People

Phishing simulation and training by department

Ran a company-wide phishing simulation, then used what it showed to build separate sessions for finance, procurement, security operations and the executive team, each built on the scams that department actually runs into.

Finance covered fake supplier bank-detail changes and boss impersonation. Procurement covered fake vendors and tender fraud. Leadership got the business risk, not the jargon.

Consulting · Public sector

Penetration test of a national railway operator

Tested the external perimeter, the internal corporate network, the operational rail network and public web systems, following OSSTMM, PTES, NIST SP 800-115 and OWASP.

Confirmed 13 vulnerabilities, including end-of-life Windows servers still open to EternalBlue-class attacks. Delivered an executive report and a separate technical report so both audiences could act on it.

Consulting · Payments

PCI DSS for payment providers and fintechs

Assessed payment service providers and fintechs in Kenya, Rwanda and Nigeria against PCI DSS, alongside web, API and mobile app testing.

Turned findings into remediation plans that engineering teams could work through and managers could track toward compliance.
